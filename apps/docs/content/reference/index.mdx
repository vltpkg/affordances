The guides explain how the pieces fit. These pages are what to look things up in.

- [**Protocol**](/docs/reference/protocol): the normative Affordances protocol, version 0. What a graph, an action call, and a delta look like on the wire.
- [**Options**](/docs/reference/options): every option of `defineAction`, `defineState`, `createAffordances`, `createHandler`, and `withAffordances`, in one place.
- [**CLI**](/docs/reference/cli): `affordances init` and `build` to set an app up, and `open` and `call` to see and use a page the way an agent does.
- [**API reference**](/docs/reference/api-reference): every package's exports and signatures, generated from source with TypeDoc.
