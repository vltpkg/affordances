The frontmatter `title` is the page's `#` heading when the `<title>` is that heading in the site's template, such as `Authentication — Acme Docs` for `<h1>Authentication</h1>`, with the heading on either side of a `·`, `|`, `–`, `—` or `-`. Any other `<title>` stays as written.
